# Decisions MOC

A log of why key configuration and design choices were made. Use this to remember your reasoning when you revisit settings months later.

> [!note] Why keep a decision log?
> Config values like thresholds and provider choices feel obvious when you set them, but the reasoning fades. Recording it here saves you from re-litigating the same question on every revisit.
